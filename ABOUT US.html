Beyond the Rank.

Buslink Botswana was born from a simple observation: Botswana's heart beats on the road, yet the way we access travel remained manual. We are a tech-driven logistics partner dedicated to digitizing the journey from Gaborone to the Okavango.

No more waking up at 4:00 AM to stand in line at the Gaborone Bus Rank. We provide a transparent, secure, and luxury-focused booking engine for the modern Motswana traveler.

Safety Is Our Standard

Traveling at night requires expertise. We don't just sell tickets; we audit our partners for your peace of mind.

🛡️ Fleet Integrity

Every operator is required to submit up-to-date Roadworthiness Certificates and 'P' Permits regularly.

📞 Journey Support

Our team monitors major routes. In case of delays, Buslink users are notified first via SMS.

🐆 Wildlife Awareness

We prioritize operators with dual-driver systems for night routes to ensure maximum alertness.
